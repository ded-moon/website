About me

Hi, I'm ded_moon! :3

I'm a Romanian tinkerer and aspiring engineer currently studying at the Faculty of Industrial Engineering, Robotics and Production Management (FIIRMP) at Technical University of Cluj-Napoca (UTCN). I'm also a member of SpicyVoltage, a crew of tech enthusiasts with whom I participate in robotics competitions, CTFs, hackathons, and programming contests.

A significant part of my experience comes from FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC). I've participated in multiple seasons as a member of the Mechanical Department of DarkEnergy and DecebalTech, working on the side of robot development. My experience includes mechanical design, fabrication, assembly, maintenance, troubleshooting, and preparing robots for competition, alongside additional responsibilities in photography, public relations and event organisation.

I later returned as a peer mentor, where I was able to help younger team members develop their engineering and problem-solving skills. Beyond working on the robot itself, FTC also taught me a great deal about teamwork, engineering under time constraints, competition strategy and documentation. As a peer mentor, I also helped organize the Western Region FTC competition.

  • Oracle Academy - Database Design - completed the Database Design course.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection between software and hardware: embedded systems, robotics, electronics and engineering in general. I enjoy both building things from scratch and figuring out how existing systems work - preferably by taking them apart first. :3

Community & volunteering

I've volunteered throughout all of my high-school years at European Researchers' Night, an event organized by my school, helping with the activities and demonstrations presented to visitors. I've also contributed to various events and activities organized through our robotics club.

Outside of school and competitions, I contribute to OpenStreetMap, including through tools and platforms such as StreetComplete, MapSwipe, and the HOT Tasking Manager. I also regularly participate in citizen-science projects through Zooniverse.

Through these projects, instead of building something for a competition, I get to contribute small pieces of useful data and work on projects that can benefit other people and humanity as a whole.
